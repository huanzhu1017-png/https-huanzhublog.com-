How I usually do it

  1. Follow the number around. Who sends what to whom, and when. Most of the error happens in the hand-offs.

  2. Pull from the source. Get pipeline and actuals straight from the ERP, so nobody re-types anything.

  3. Give AI the boring parts. Checking data, flagging weird stuff, writing first drafts, ranking what needs attention. People still make the calls.

  4. Make it the only version. Roll it out with the business, switch off the old process, and keep score on accuracy every month.
