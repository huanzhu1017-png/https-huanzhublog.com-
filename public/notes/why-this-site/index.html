Why I made this site

For a long time, almost everything I made lived inside a company: dashboards, models, decks. They did their job, and then they stayed behind a login I'd eventually lose.

So I wanted a small place on the internet that's mine. Somewhere I can show what I've built, write down what I've learned, and keep a few pieces of life that don't fit on a résumé.

The rules I'm giving myself

  • Nothing confidential. Work pieces get rebuilt with made-up numbers.
  • Short is fine. A note can be three paragraphs.
  • It doesn't have to be finished. It'll grow slowly.
If you're reading this, hello. Thanks for stopping by.
