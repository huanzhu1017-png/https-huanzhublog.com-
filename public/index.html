Hi, I'm Huan.

I grew up in Beijing and have lived in New York for over 12 years. This is my notebook.

By day I work in finance, with over 12 years of experience. I like turning messy processes into simple ones, and I like a forecast that turns out to be right. The rest of the time I'm figuring out the city, reading, and trying new things.

This site is where I keep a bit of all of it: things I've built, things I'm thinking about, and pieces of everyday life. It'll fill up slowly.
