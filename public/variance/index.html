Letting AI write the first draft of variance commentary

Before

Explaining budget vs. actual took two weeks every month: pull the data, chase business leaders for context, then write the commentary by hand. By the time it was done, people were already asking about the next month.

What I built

A variance dashboard that splits the gap between budget and actual into its drivers. AI agents pull the supporting detail for each driver and draft a comment explaining it.

The team reviews and edits those drafts instead of starting from a blank page. People still decide what the story is; the agents do the digging.

What happened

The reporting and analysis cycle went from two weeks to two days.
